## Method
- `verify_sm103.py` (from B2) with two arms per cell: cuBLAS (`F.linear`) and the given config. The "proposed" input was empty, so the config shows up as the `sm100` column in `verify.jsonl`.
- 4 seeds × 3 rounds, order rotating per round, CUPTI + CUDA graph + cold L2, `repeat_time_ms=100`. Per seed the middle round is kept; per cell, the median over seeds of the per-seed speedup is reported.
- 8 processes, one per GPU: GPUs 0–3 ran dense seeds 0–3, GPUs 4–7 ran MoE seeds 0–3. The node was idle (no other GPU users at any 30 s check). The Qwen3-32B weight download for B5 overlapped the verify; it is host/network only.
- Inputs: `results/manager/B4/{dense_qwen3_sm100.json, qwen3_moe_sm100.json}`, unchanged. Tree e66bede56b (the skinny kernel is identical in #69736).
